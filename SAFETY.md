# Safety and responsibility

[Deutsch](SAFETY.de.md)

Glucose Voice Alarm is experimental proof-of-concept software for personal research and information. It is a Beta, has not undergone medical-device certification or regulatory approval, and is not a certified medical device. Availability of its source code and passing synthetic tests do not establish clinical safety or reliability.

Do not use or rely on the app or its documentation for medical purposes, diagnosis, treatment or dosing decisions. It does not replace professional healthcare judgement. Ask a qualified healthcare professional about medical questions.

Do not rely on this app for real-time alarms, emergency monitoring or any time-critical information. Alarms, speech, vibration, notifications, readings and trend recognition can be absent, wrong, duplicated or delayed. Any part of the system can fail at any time, including without a useful error message. Silence does not indicate that glucose is safe.

Follow the instructions supplied with your glucose sensor, pump, reader, receiver and other equipment. Continue to use their prescribed monitoring and safety functions. Do not discontinue an accompanying reader or receiver, or change prescribed therapy, because this companion is installed. CamAPS remains the sensor/pump controller; this app neither validates nor replaces it.

The app reads notifications, which can change or disappear. It uses receipt time rather than a confirmed measurement timestamp. It cannot recover missing sensor readings. Its gap warning reports the last known context and does not prove that a trend has continued, stopped or accelerated. A displayed or spoken value is not an independently verified measurement.
