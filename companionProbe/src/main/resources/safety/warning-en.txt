Glucose Voice Alarm is an experimental beta. Not for medical use – research tool only. The app is not a certified medical device and has no medical regulatory approval.

Do not use readings, trends or alarms for diagnosis, treatment or dosing decisions. They do not replace medical advice or the prescribed functions of your sensor, pump or other medical devices.

Readings, arrows, sounds, speech and vibration may be missing, wrong, delayed or repeated. The app may fail without a visible error. Silence does not mean safety. Do not rely on this app for emergencies or time-critical situations.

GVA reads CamAPS notifications. Their structure and availability may change. Receipt time is not a verified measurement time. Missing readings are not replaced with estimates; the last trend does not establish continuation or acceleration.

CamAPS remains responsible for the sensor and pump. Keep using your prescribed devices, checks and medical procedures. GVA does not validate or replace them.
